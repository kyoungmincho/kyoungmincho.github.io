Kyoungmin Cho Research CV

Kyoungmin Cho

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor of Philosophy at Mount Holyoke College. I received my Ph.D. in Philosophy from University of Illinois Chicago in 2024.

I work primarily in philosophy of action and ethics, and at the intersection of the two. My research focuses on the purposive structure of intentional action: the role of ends in practical reasoning and action explanation, the nature of acting for its own sake, and the connections among agency, practical knowledge, and value. I also have research interests in the history of philosophy, especially Aristotle and Kant.

My current research project investigates ultimate ends in practical reasoning: whether practical reasoning requires such ends, what makes an end ultimate, and how actions done for their own sake might provide them.
